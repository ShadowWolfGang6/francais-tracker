The five-step lesson

Use this workflow for an A2 activity. It also works at B1 and B2.

Listen first — no transcript

Watch or listen once. Identify the topic, speakers and general message. Don't pause for every unknown word.

Answer the comprehension questions

Use what you understood. Guess where necessary; this trains real-time comprehension.

Check the transcript

Review incorrect answers. Find 3–5 useful words or expressions, not every unfamiliar item.

Listen again

Replay a short section while following the French text, then listen once more without reading.

Produce something

Say or write 2–3 original sentences using expressions from the lesson.

Long-term direction

From A2 toward B2.

Move forward when your abilities are ready, not just when a calendar says so.

Current focus
A2

Build the base

  • Everyday conversations
  • Past, present and future
  • Clear, slow speech
  • Short connected writing

Ready to progress when: you can discuss familiar topics and understand the main points of clear everyday audio.

Next milestone
B1

Become independent

  • Longer conversations
  • Explain opinions
  • Follow learner podcasts
  • Read articles on familiar topics

Ready to progress when: you can manage most familiar situations and explain experiences and viewpoints.

Advanced development
B1+

Build range

  • Native interviews
  • Abstract topics
  • Quebec French exposure
  • Structured arguments

Focus: reduce dependence on subtitles and become more precise and spontaneous.

Target
B2

Confident use

  • Complex discussions
  • Professional communication
  • Extended listening
  • Detailed writing

Target ability: interact with reasonable fluency and spontaneity across a broad range of subjects.

At 20 minutes per day, expect this to be a multi-year project. Use CEFR skill descriptors to assess listening, reading, spoken interaction, spoken production and writing separately. A completed lesson or app streak is not proof of a CEFR level.
